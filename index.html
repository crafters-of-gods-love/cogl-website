Discord Rules

Discord Server Rules

These are our discord server's rules. Not following them could lead to a ban. Attempting to find loopholes will not be tolerated.

01NO SWEARING

We do not permit any sort of swearing on our server.

- This includes replacement words such as frick, fricking, or fudge.

- Words such as heck, darn and dang are okay.

02KEEP CHAT CLEAN

Do not text or post any memes, photos or videos that are nude, crass (gross, or inappropriate), gory (bloody, graphic), horror, inappropriate or suggestive. This also applies for usernames, profile pictures and custom status. Visual potty humor is not allowed. Chatting about potty humor is okay, but please do not be descriptive.

- This includes swim attire and basic modesty.

Modesty for men: shorts to mid-thigh.

Modesty for women: chest and tummy covered, shorts or skirts to mid-thigh.

- This includes crude potty humor.

- This includes feet.

03RESPECT OTHERS

Please be respectful in the way you interact with other users on the server. Respect staff and their decisions, even if you disagree with them.

- If a staff member asks you to move on or stop something please listen to them.

- Treat others the way you would want to be treated.

Ephesians 4:29 says, "Do not let any unwholesome talk come out of your mouths, but only what is helpful for building others up according to their needs, that it may benefit those who listen."

- Encourage one another. Use your words wisely, for they can build others up, but also tear others down.

04FIGHTS/DEBATES

Any sort of fighting or debates that break out must be discussed in a calm manner. Insults thrown into the mix is not tolerated and will be dealt with. The argument/fight may be shut down as this is not a debate server.

- This includes throwing digs at each other. If you cannot argue respectfully do not argue.

05NO GOSSIPING

Gossip only creates hurt. We're not going to gossip about others here.

- This includes talking about other's punishments. Don't talk about others wrong doings and flaws behind their back. This includes insulting/namecalling people behind their back. Matthew 7:4.

06BE NICE TO EACH OTHER

Be nice to each other; no rude teasing, drama or starting arguments (for the wrath of man does not produce the righteousness of God. -James 1:20) be an example of Christ. Playful comments are fine but please do not let it become anything serious or offensive. (Ephesians 5:4)

- Bringing up past conflicts is included in this rule.

- Our server is no place for people who wish to cause division and conflict. You will be removed from the server if you cause drama.

07NO SHARING PRIVATE INFORMATION

No sharing private information. Private information such as email, name, phone number, address, where you live, etc, should stay private. (Age is fine for you to share, though we don't suggest it.) We want you to stay safe online!

- If you know someone in real life who's online please be safe and do not share their information.

- Don't share other people's personal information please.

08NO ADVERTISING

No advertising. We do not allow promotion of any kind. This includes mentioning other servers and promoting/mentioning any other sort of media.

- We allow YouTube and Medal links to be shared on our server, but please make sure they abide by our rules and are 5 minutes or shorter to respect moderator's time. If you have a concern about sharing something please ask staff before sharing it.

- If a meme has a link in it then it falls under this rule and you should not post it. Or you can crop it out. Exceptions to this rule are websites imgflip, ifunny and imgplay.

09SPAMMING AND CAPS

We allow spamming and caps, but don't do it too often. You'll be told by a staff member to stop if you do it too much.

- We determine spamming by how much space it takes up on the screen.

10NO INAPPROPRIATE CONVERSATIONS

Any sort of inappropriate talk is prohibited. There are certain things that should not be discussed on our server. This is because we have younger users who play and we want to keep them safe.

- Inappropriate conversations can consist of talking about sexual things, nudity, graphic images, drug talk, alcohol, and other things. Potty humor is fine as long as it is not descriptive.

- Joking about furries and LGBTQ+ related content is not permitted. Serious discussions about this topic should be kept in DMs or serious chat on Discord.

- Please don't role play as or pretend to be other genders. Example: boy pretending to be a girl. This isn't appropriate because of our current culture.

- Please do not "rizz" or "ship" users on our server. We don't want users to feel uncomfortable here or feel harassed.

- We do not allow public conversations about suicide due to the sensitivity of this topic. If you are in crisis, please contact the suicide prevention hotline in your area. The number for the hotline in the United States is 988. Please refer to the following document for guidelines and rules concerning the discussion of suicide on our server.

https://drive.google.com/file/d/1TjH-5Ijm0-eFsjyRya3OKCb-mto7Rgtb/view?usp=sharing

- By participating in this community, you agree to abide by these guidelines to maintain a safe and supportive environment for all users.

11NO UPLOADING FILES

Please do not upload files. We do not want people downloading viruses.

- If you upload something that is not previewable, then it will be deleted as it goes against our rules.

12ENGLISH ONLY

We ask that you only speak English on the server as using other languages makes it hard to moderate and keep everyone safe. This rule includes any sort of code languages too.

- Common greetings and commonly known things such as "hola" are okay. We won't allow full sentences or conversations in other languages. If we don't know what is said then we may delete what is posted.

13JOKING

If a joke causes another person to feel hurt please don't joke with them that way. Be respectful and understand they don't want to joke that way. If you feel hurt by a joke then please say something to the person so they know in the future not to joke with you in that way.

14NO BYPASSING PUNISMENTS OR HAVING ALTS

We do not allow alt accounts on our server. If you use an alt account on our server you will be banned. Bypassing punishments through an alt, or using another person's account to interact on the server will lead all involved accounts to being banned from the server.

15Discussing Plugins/Bots

We do not allow our plugins on our Minecraft server to be discussed publicly. Same can be said for bots on the Discord server. We want to have privacy regarding them, some of them keep the server safe and knowledge of them would pose a risk. Please don't discuss them. This includes their name, code, use, etc. Thanks for respecting our privacy.

16Ayeon Character Rule

You may only role play as a single character in Ayeon. You may not role play as multiple people. If you want to switch characters or throw away your current character and create a new one, you will need to talk to an admin regarding that.

17MAGIC IN AYEON

We do not allow magic to be role played in our role play.

General Help

What is in-game money used for?

Money is used for rank upgrades and player trading. Earn it by playing, mining ores, defeating mobs, attending events, and completing Skyblock challenges. Check your balance with /bal.

How do I unlock Creative?

The Creative world becomes available after reaching the Merchant rank.

How do I play Skyblock?

COGL has a Skyblock tutorial video covering the basics.

Open the Skyblock tutorial →

How do I rank up?

Run /kits, then select the orange dye labeled “ranks” to view and purchase available rank upgrades.

Is duping enabled?

No. TNT duping, sand duping, and other duplication methods are disabled.

Our Purpose

A safe Christian community built to protect and encourage.

COGL is a community that is primarily oriented towards Christian minors (13-17), whom we as staff weigh our decisions on moderation and other policies for.

A welcome from Sir Silliness

Welcome to COGL! My name (Minecraft name) is Sir Silliness. I am the owner and founder of Crafters of God's Love. This community is for Christians and families to gather together, connect virtually, encourage one another, and show each other the love Jesus showed us. Below is our purpose statement to give you some more background on the purpose of our server. We hope you enjoy our server and all it has to offer!

01

Why this focus exists

This decision was made because there is a greater need for a safe Christian group for this demographic, and because previous conflicts regarding moderation were rooted in a lack of a clear designated purpose.

Often times among staff and members it boiled down into unofficial factions of “Make COGL a place that is catered more to adults by allowing X and Y” and “Make COGL a place that is catered and safer for minors by removing X and Y.”

02

Why our rules are stricter

Because of this decision, we handle things differently than other Christian Discord servers that may be looser on certain issues. We don’t allow links, websites, files, and other things that are generally considered acceptable in other servers primarily because the target demographic of our server is minors.

Of course, we want adults to be safe too, but certain policies would be changed if our target demographic was 18+.

03

Protection and edification

We believe we are called to provide an especially safe and stable environment for minors, both from threats such as hackers, scammers, and other malicious individuals alongside protecting their minds by giving them an example of how to live as believers.

Protecting means we want this place to be of good moral standing by avoiding certain things, even if considered “minor,” such as degrading jokes and memes.

Our vision for COGL is to keep it as a space that not only protects minors from dangerous individuals or toxic environments, but to edify them.

04

Staff conviction

Unanimously, we as staff have decided that we don’t want to see certain things. We are not paid to do our jobs, and the last thing we want to deal with in our precious free time is to allow things we think are degrading to witness both as adults and especially for minors.

We are all well aware of the frustrations that members have had with stricter moderation. Even among staff, there have been many intense discussions as to what policies are considered unnecessary. However, we are fully convinced that this is the right direction for COGL, even if we are not perfect in all of our policies.

05

A message to adult members

For users who are 18+, we want you to know you are always welcomed to join, assuming you follow the rules. In the past, we know some who were older had difficulties with COGL’s rules, and thus there have been arguments over our policies.

So, our official message from staff to anyone who has difficulty with this is to please go to other servers that allow the content you want. Do not try to turn COGL into something that it is not, as in, an adult 18+ hang out group. Thank you.

As staff, we are exceptionally grateful for all our current COGL members who follow the rules and want to enjoy good clean fun and give joy and happiness to others! We understand certain rules can be annoying, so that makes us all the more grateful for how respectful you guys are!

May God’s love, joy, and peace be with you all! Thank you for understanding! — COGL staff

What We Believe

One faith. One confession. One community.

COGL embraces the Nicene Creed as our foundational statement of faith. Originating in the fourth century, the Nicene Creed was crafted by early Christians as a defining and unifying declaration of belief for the entire body of Christ.

Statement of Faith

A shared confession of worship

Over the centuries, the Nicene Creed has served as a lasting benchmark for what Christians across various traditions and denominations affirm.

The Nicene Creed is not simply a summary of doctrines; it is a shared confession of worship that shapes our identity as God’s people and unites us in the life of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. This is what we believe.

Our foundational statement

We believe in one God, the Father, the Almighty, Maker of heaven and earth, of all that is, seen and unseen.

We believe in one Lord, Jesus Christ, the only Son of God, eternally begotten of the Father, God from God, Light from Light, true God from true God, begotten, not made, of one being with the Father, through him all things were made; for us and for our salvation he came down from heaven, was incarnate of the Virgin Mary, and became man; for our sake he was crucified under Pontius Pilate, suffered, and was buried; on the third day he rose again in accordance with the Scriptures; he ascended into heaven and sits at the right hand of the Father.

He will come again in glory to judge the living and the dead, and his kingdom will have no end.

We believe in the Holy Spirit, the Lord, the giver of life, who proceeds from the Father; with the Father and Son he is worshipped and glorified; he has spoken through the prophets. We believe in one, holy, and apostolic Church. We acknowledge one baptism for the forgiveness of sins. We look for the resurrection of the dead and the life of the world to come.

“In essentials, unity; in non-essentials, liberty; in all things, charity.”
— St. Augustine of Hippo
